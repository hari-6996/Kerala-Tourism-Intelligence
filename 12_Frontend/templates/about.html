Project Documentation

Kerala Tourism Destination Analysis & Business Opportunity Identification

Using unsupervised K-Means clustering, infrastructure gap scoring, and visitor trends across 25 benchmark corridors in Kerala.

01. Problem Statement & Research Objectives

Hospitality investment in Kerala concentrates heavily around well-known centers such as Munnar, Kochi, and Kovalam. Meanwhile, developing nature and cultural hubs receive rising visitor interest without proportional local infrastructure. This study compares recorded establishment supply within similar destination clusters to identify relative gaps.

  • Mature-hub congestion: High property saturation coupled with seasonal strain in established destinations.
  • Emerging-corridor gaps: Lower recorded homestay or restaurant counts in growing secondary hubs.
Multi-Source Dataset Synthesis

Compiled commercial counts, customer ratings, 2022–2024 footfall, search interest, and connectivity across 25 hubs.

Unsupervised K-Means Segmentation

Grouped destinations into 3 peer clusters using StandardScaler, RobustScaler, and PCA evaluation.

Cluster-Relative Gap Scoring

Measured sector-wise differences against cluster peer averages alongside 3-year visitor trends.

Sensitivity & ROI Calculator

Interactive break-even and ROI estimator driven by user-supplied capital and operating inputs.

02. Dataset Architecture & Preprocessing

Domain Source Scope Primary Variables
Hospitality Supply Verified directory & public listings Hotels, Resorts, Homestays, Restaurants
Public Ratings Aggregated visitor reviews (1–5 scale) Sector mean ratings across accommodation & dining
Visitor Footfall Kerala Tourism published statistics & estimates Domestic, Foreign, and Total Visits (2022–2024)
Search Trends Google Trends India (monthly index) Destination, stay, food, and activity interest scores
Transit Access Highway & transport distance tables Airport distance (km), railway stations, highway access

Preprocessing steps included normalizing typographic dashes across destination names, parsing string distances to numeric kilometers, and flagging estimated visitor figures separately from official records.

04. Gap Formulas & Scope Limitations

Gap(i, sector) = Mean_Cluster(sector) - Count(i, sector)
Overall Gap Score(i) = 0.25 * (Hotel_Gap + Resort_Gap + Homestay_Gap + Restaurant_Gap)
Monthly Profit = Monthly Revenue - Monthly Expenses
Annual Profit = Monthly Profit * 12
Payback Period (Months) = Initial Investment / Monthly Profit
Annual ROI (%) = (Annual Profit / Initial Investment) * 100

Key Limitations & Future Scope

  • Infrastructure gaps capture relative differences in recorded business counts and do not prove market demand.
  • Remote eco-tourism sanctuaries without automated counters rely on provisional estimates rather than audited totals.
  • Profitability simulations rely strictly on user-supplied capital and operating estimates.
