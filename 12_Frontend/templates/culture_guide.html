Seasonal Tourism & Cultural Calendar

Three primary inflow windows
Oct – Mar · Peak Season

Coastal & Cultural Influx

Cool dry weather across Varkala and Kovalam beaches, Munnar and Vagamon hill retreats, North Malabar Theyyam shrines, and Fort Kochi art festivals.

Apr – May · Summer Escape

Highland Tea & Mist Retreats

Domestic travelers shift to cooler high-altitude estates and grasslands including Munnar, Wayanad, Nelliyampathy, Ponmudi, and Ranipuram.

Jun – Sep · Karkidakam & Onam

Monsoon Wellness & Boat Races

Ayurvedic monsoon treatments and peak waterfall flows at Athirappilly and Palaruvi, culminating in late-summer Onam festivities and boat regattas.
